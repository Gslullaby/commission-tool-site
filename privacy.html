Privacy Policy

Operator: Xiaofu Technology Team · Effective date: September 4, 2026

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the E-commerce Commission Tool (the “Tool”) processes data used for livestream host and video editor commission calculations. The Tool is a Windows desktop application operated by the Xiaofu Technology Team.

This informational website does not provide user registration, use advertising trackers, or receive users' Excel files, order records, or commission results.

2. Data Processed by the Tool

Local business files

The Tool processes files actively selected by the user, including host schedules, order details, video registration lists, and commission settings. It also creates filtered order files, order-to-host matching records, GMV summaries, verification evidence, exception reports, and commission results.

TikTok account and video data

After explicit authorization by a TikTok account holder, the Tool retrieves basic account information and video-list data through official TikTok APIs. This may include the display name, account identifier, video identifier, video title, publication time, and cumulative view count.

The Tool requests the TikTok permissions user.info.basic and video.list.

Authorization credentials

The Tool processes the TikTok Client Key and Client Secret configured by the app operator, together with access tokens and refresh tokens issued after account authorization.

3. Purposes of Processing

Data is processed to:

  • filter eligible livestream orders and match them to host work periods;
  • calculate GMV and commissions by host and livestream account;
  • confirm that a registered video belongs to an authorized TikTok account;
  • verify publication dates, cumulative view counts, and reward eligibility;
  • create intermediate evidence and final results for operational and financial review;
  • refresh TikTok authorization when permitted; and
  • diagnose errors and maintain the security and reliability of the Tool.

4. Local Storage and Network Transmission

Source Excel files, intermediate evidence, configuration outputs, and commission results are processed and stored locally in folders selected by the user or created by the Tool. They are not uploaded to servers operated by the Xiaofu Technology Team.

The TikTok Client Secret, access tokens, and refresh tokens are stored in encrypted local storage scoped to the current Windows user.

Network communication occurs when the user opens TikTok's official authorization service, exchanges or refreshes authorization tokens, or requests account and video data from official TikTok API servers. TikTok processes information it receives according to its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell personal data. We do not use local business files for advertising. The operator does not intentionally receive the user's local source files or calculation outputs. Data is sent to TikTok only as required to complete authorization and retrieve the account or video information requested by the user.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process, but only to the extent legally required and only if the operator actually possesses that information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The user controls the retention of local business files and calculation outputs and may delete them at any time. The user may also delete the Tool's local encrypted authorization storage and local configuration files.

A TikTok account holder may revoke the Tool's access through the relevant TikTok account settings. TikTok may retain authorization or access records under its own policies.

7. User Choices and Rights

  • You may use the local livestream commission workflow without connecting a TikTok account.
  • You may decline or revoke TikTok authorization, after which TikTok-dependent features will no longer work.
  • You may delete local configurations, credentials, evidence files, and calculation results.
  • You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information that the operator actually holds, subject to applicable law.

8. Data Security

The Tool uses Windows user-scoped encryption to protect locally stored TikTok Client Secrets and authorization tokens. Users remain responsible for protecting their Windows account, device, source files, and output folders. Client Secrets, tokens, and business workbooks should not be shared publicly.

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. Users should promptly revoke TikTok access and remove local credentials if they believe their device or account has been compromised.

9. Children's Privacy

The Tool is intended for e-commerce operations and finance personnel. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Tool.

10. International and Third-Party Processing

TikTok services may process account and API data in locations determined by TikTok. Users should review TikTok's privacy disclosures before authorizing an account. The Tool does not control TikTok's independent processing practices.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Tool's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised effective date will be displayed at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Xiaofu Technology Team through its public GitHub profile: github.com/Gslullaby.
